# Public View of the Volume Management System

## How The Public Sees Your Journal Volumes

Your volume management system provides a **professional, academic-standard public interface** that gives readers and researchers multiple ways to access your published content. Here's exactly how the public will experience your journal:

## 🌐 Public Access Routes

### 1. Main Archive Landing Page
**URL:** `/archive`
- **Experience:** Professional journal homepage with search functionality
- **Features:**
  - Search across all articles and volumes
  - Filter by categories, years, and volumes
  - Quick links to browse volumes
  - Publication statistics
  - Advanced search capabilities

### 2. Volumes Directory
**URL:** `/archive/volumes`
- **Experience:** Clean, organized view of all published volumes
- **Features:**
  - Grid and list view options
  - Volume statistics (articles, views, downloads)
  - Search and filter by year
  - Category breakdown
  - Professional volume cards with preview information

### 3. Individual Volume Pages
**URL:** `/archive/volume/[number]` (e.g., `/archive/volume/1`)
- **Experience:** Dedicated page for each volume with full academic formatting
- **Features:**
  - Professional volume header with statistics
  - Complete article listings with abstracts
  - Author information and affiliations
  - DOI links and citation tools
  - Download options for PDFs
  - Social sharing capabilities
  - Academic citation formats (APA, MLA, Chicago)

```

MLA Format:

Author, First Name. "Article Title." Advances in Medicine and Health Sciences Journal 1 (2025): pages. https://doi.org/xx.xxxx/xxxxx.
```
